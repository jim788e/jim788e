# No Em Dash (`—`) in User-Facing Copy

In user-facing copy (taglines, marketing sentences, documentation, i18n strings, and READMEs), do NOT use the em dash `—` (U+2014) as a sentence or clause separator. Rewrite with a comma, period, colon, or restructured phrasing instead.

### Examples
- ❌ `"Thoughtful service — tailored to you."`
- ✅ `"Thoughtful service, tailored to you."` / `"Thoughtful service. Tailored to you."`

### Exceptions
**Keep** the en dash `–` (U+2013) when it denotes a range / from–to (e.g., `"10:00–19:00"`, `"2024–2026"`, `"−20%"` offers).

### Application
When writing or editing any human-language copy, avoid `—`. When cleaning existing copy, search for `—` specifically (not `–`) and replace it with natural punctuation.
